# ExportDesk — Export operations software

**B2B SaaS · Workflow design · Document systems**

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md)

## Product challenge

Export teams often manage buyer inquiries, quotation revisions, pricing, approvals, documents, and customer follow-ups across disconnected tools. That makes it difficult to understand what is complete, what needs review, and what may be sent.

## Project overview

ExportDesk brings exporter workflows into a structured workspace, from an initial inquiry through quotation preparation, approvals, trade document generation, and follow-up. It is a software product, not a logistics operator.

## Selected implementation work

- Structured inquiry review and supporting evidence.
- Quotation creation with cost, pricing, margin, and approval states.
- Document preparation and branded PDF workflows.
- Controlled communication and follow-up experiences.
- Responsive workspace UX and automated workflow/browser testing.

**Technology:** React, JavaScript, Node.js, Express, PDF tooling, Playwright.

## Current status

The advanced application is maintained in a private development branch and has not been approved for public production release. No customer-specific pricing, documents, or private code are shared here.

**What this project demonstrates:** making operational complexity manageable through product architecture, explicit business rules, design iteration, and testable workflows.
